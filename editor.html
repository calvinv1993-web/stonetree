Mouse & keys

  • Left: use tool · Right: erase
  • Middle drag / Space+drag / WASD: pan
  • Wheel: zoom
  • PgUp PgDn: change floor
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo / redo
  • Select tool: Ctrl+C copy, Ctrl+X cut, Ctrl+V paste at the mouse, Del delete

Upper floors are drawn one tile up-left of the floor below, like in the game: a roof for a house on floor 4 is painted on floor 5 on the same coordinates.

Stairs/ladders go up, holes/stairs down go down. Put a hole above a ladder (or stairs down above stairs up) to make it two-way.

The yellow flag is where new characters start; the blue flag is where they arrive after choosing a vocation.

Teleports, levers, signs and special doors ask for their setting when placed (cyan dot). Houses are green outlines: drag one out with the House tool, then put a house door in its wall.

NPC dialogs, shops, quests, items and monsters are edited in the data editor.
