# Skill trees: the plan

**Built for the Hawk** (the other paths still learn their spells by level). The plan, as approved: The idea comes from tiered skill trees in older MMOs
(lines of skills that grow stronger tier by tier, points you spend yourself), made simpler and fit for a phone. Every
name, number and picture here is our own.

## 1. The rules

- **Four lines per path, three tiers each.** A line is one kind of skill (a hard single shot, an area attack, a helper,
  a buff). Its tiers are stronger versions of it, opened by your level: **tier 1 at level 10, tier 2 at 25, tier 3 at
  45.** Tiers 4 (level 65) and 5 (level 85) come later, with the levelling to 100.
- **One button per line, and it grows.** A line is one button on the action bar, whatever its tier: when you learn tier
  2, the same button casts the stronger skill, with its own picture. Four lines fit the phone's action bar beside the
  potions and food.
- **Ranks.** Each tier has **3 ranks**. A rank makes it stronger (about +15% power each). The next tier opens once the
  one before is at rank 3 and your level is high enough.
- **Skill points.** **1 point a level from level 10** (when you choose your path), and one more for each of a few big
  quests. **A rank costs its tier's number in points**: tier 1 ranks cost 1, tier 2 ranks cost 2, tier 3 ranks cost 3.
  So a whole line to tier 3 costs 18 points, all four 72, and by level 45 you have about 36: you choose which lines
  come first. At level 100 (with tiers 4 and 5) you still can't have everything.
- **Two passives per path**, 5 ranks each at 1 point a rank (a small lasting bonus per rank).
- **Weapons.** Some lines need a weapon in your hands (a Hawk's shots need a bow or crossbow, a Bastion's shield line a
  shield). Without it the button is greyed, with a word why.
- **Starting over.** In the tree's window (for now; later at your path's teacher): the first time free, after that gold (your level x 50).
- **What stays as it is:** the spells everyone has (Torchlight, Light Heal, Cure Poison, Haste), the conjuring spells
  (arrows, bolts, runes: learned by level, as now, outside the tree) and Final Blaze (said, never on a button).

## 2. The paths

The level is when the tier opens. Mana goes up with the tier. Existing spells are reused where they fit (in brackets:
what each one is now).

### Bastion: stands in front and takes the blows (melee weapon; line 3 needs a shield)

| Line | Tier 1 (level 10) | Tier 2 (level 25) | Tier 3 (level 45) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blow** (one target) | Heavy Blow (Power Strike) | Cleaving Blow: + bleeding | Sundering Blow: ignores half its armour, stuns a moment |
| **Sweep** (all around you) | Whirlwind | Iron Arc: a half circle in front, 2 tiles (Circle Throw) | Earthshaker: radius 2, knocks back |
| **Shield** (needs a shield) | Shield Bash: stuns (Shield Bash) | Bulwark: half the damage you take, 8 s | Wall of Iron: Bulwark for friends next to you too |
| **War Cry** | Challenge: monsters near you turn on you | Battle Cry: + attack for 20 s | Last Stand: below a third of your health, heal over 10 s |

Passives: **Thick Skin** (+3% health a rank), **Steady Arm** (+2% chance to block a rank).

### Hawk: strikes from afar (lines 1 and 2 need a bow or crossbow)

| Line | Tier 1 (level 10) | Tier 2 (level 25) | Tier 3 (level 45) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shot** (one line) | Power Shot (Power Shot) | Gale Shot: further, through more | Sky Lance: and a burst where it stops |
| **Volley** (many) | Double Shot: 2 arrows, a red trail | Triple Shot: 3 arrows, a red trail | Quad Shot: 4 blood-red arrows (and at level 65 a fourth tier, Penta Shot: 5, darker, sparks and bursts) |

Passives: **Sharp Eye** (+2% critical hit chance a rank), **Light Feet** (+2% speed a rank). Greater Heal stays a Hawk

### Ember: burns whole packs at once (no weapon needed)

## 3. On the phone

- **The window.** Opened from the drawer (a new "Skills" tab, beside the spells). A full screen: the free points at the
  top ("3 points"), the four lines below each other, each a row of three big icons (the tiers), and the two passives
  under them. On an icon: its rank (2/3), a lock with "Lv 25" while it's shut, a gold edge when a rank can be bought.
- **Tap an icon:** a card slides up at the bottom: what it does now and at the next rank, its mana and cooldown, what it
  needs (level, weapon), and one big button: **Learn (2 points)**.
- **A dot** on the drawer's tab and on the guide when you have points to spend, as for new quests.
- **The action bar.** A line's button shows its highest tier's picture and casts that; nothing to drag. A line with
  nothing learned isn't on the bar.
- **The computer** gets the same window, with the mouse.

## 4. In the code

- **Data** (`creatures.js`): a `TREES` table per vocation, its lines, tiers, passives and costs, pointing at entries in
  `SPELLS`. A spell gets `line`, `tier` and how its power grows a rank. The kinds `castSpell` knows (bolt, area, wave,
  beam, strike, whirl, arc, pierce, volley, chain, heal, shield, haste) do most of it; new kinds are needed for: a mark
  (more damage taken), a summon for 20 s (Clang's companion code), a short jump (Blink), buffs on yourself and friends
  (attack, damage taken, armour), healing over time, holding in place and slowing.
- **Player fields:** `skillPoints` and `skillRanks` (`{ id: rank }`): in `newPlayer`, `SAVED_FIELDS` and `SOMETIMES`.
- **Actions:** `learnRank(id)` and `resetTree()` (at the teacher). Online they work by themselves.
- **Old saves:** a character gets the points for the levels they have, and the tier-1 skills they already knew at rank 1
  for free.
- **Tests:** unit tests for the rules (costs, locks, weapons, starting over, old saves), the phone tests for the window,
  and `balance.js` and `pace.js` for the strength along the Oakvale route.
