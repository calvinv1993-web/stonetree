# ChatGPT prompts: every item, in 7 sheets

Every item in the game, the ones painted before included, in sheets of 18 to 20. Do them in this order: the first sheets are what players see first on Oakvale.

**Each time:** attach one painted picture you like as the style reference (the health potion, for example), paste the **common part** below, and then the **list** of that sheet. Send the sheet back as it comes out: it is cut up and put in the game.

## The common part (the same for every sheet)

```
Create a sprite sheet of fantasy RPG item icons on a fully TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha, no checkerboard, no paper, no frame, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines).
Layout: 5 columns, as many rows as needed, each icon centred in its own equal square cell with clear empty space between icons.
Same painterly style, lighting (from the top left) and level of detail as the attached reference item: rich hand-painted fantasy look, soft shading, a thin dark outline, muted natural colours, no glow, no text.
Each item seen at a slight 3/4 angle, filling about 80% of its cell, nothing touching the cell edges. Every icon the same scale: a dagger smaller than a battle axe, a ring small in its cell.
Order, left to right, top to bottom:
```
